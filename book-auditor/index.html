What BookPilot cannot do

  • It cannot prove who wrote a passage. Writing-pattern findings are editorial warning signs, not authorship detection.
  • It cannot confirm originality. It does not search the web or other books. You get a list of distinctive sentences to check externally; no result is ever “plagiarism-free”.
  • It does not look up live sources. Legal checks use a built-in reference to the GDPR text and well-known rulings; each finding links to the primary source. Anything time-sensitive (AI Act dates, adequacy decisions, national law) is marked “Requires further research”.
  • Plain text has no layout. Images, tables, fonts and page design can’t be assessed from pasted text.
