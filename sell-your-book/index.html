Two shares, one simple rule

You earn more when the reader is yours, and you still earn when we do the finding.

70%

Your own audience

When the reader arrives through you — your link, newsletter, social posts, podcast, ads or events — you keep 70% of the sale.

35%

Readers BookPilot brings

When a reader finds your book through BookPilot — our catalogue, blog and recommendations — you keep 35% of the sale, for readers you did not have to find yourself.

How each share is calculated (the price it applies to, payment fees, VAT and refunds) and when you are paid are set out in the seller terms, which you agree to before you start selling. Nothing on this page promises that any book will sell.

Questions authors ask

What counts as my own audience?

Readers who come through you: your own link, your newsletter, your social posts, your ads. Readers who find your book through BookPilot itself, such as our catalogue, blog and recommendations, count as BookPilot’s. The exact rules are in section 5 of the seller terms.

Can I sell on BookPilot today?

Promoting your book is open to everyone: create an account, add your book and use the tools. Selling through BookPilot is by invitation while we open it to authors, so tell us about your book and we will onboard you.

My ebook is in KDP Select. Can I still take part?

Check Amazon’s current KDP Select terms: while an ebook is enrolled, its digital edition has to stay exclusive to Amazon. In that case link to Amazon instead of selling the ebook file here. Other editions and formats you hold the rights to are not covered by that rule, but confirm with Amazon before you decide.

Does it cost anything to start?

Creating an account and adding your book is free, with no card required. See the plans for the advertising and writing tools.

Is my manuscript used to train AI?

No. Your manuscript is never used to train any AI model, ours or anyone else’s.
