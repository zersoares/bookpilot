Seller terms for authors

For authors and creators who sell a book through BookPilot: who sells, how the 70% and 35% shares work, refunds, payouts, and what each side promises.

Effective 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

Using BookPilot to promote your book without selling it through us is covered by the Terms of service only.

2. What the words mean

  • Book: a digital book or guide you ask us to sell, and that we approve.
  • Reader: a person who buys your Book from us.
  • Price: what the Reader paid for your Book, in euros.
  • Net Amount: the Price, minus any VAT or similar sales tax contained in it, minus the payment-processing fee charged on that sale by our payment provider.
  • Own-audience sale and BookPilot-audience sale: see section 5.2.
  • Refund Window: 14 days from the day of the sale.
  • Balance: your shares of sales that have not yet been paid out.

3. Becoming a seller

3.1 By invitation

Selling through BookPilot is by invitation while we open it to authors. You create an account, add your book and tell us about it. We review it and decide whether to approve it. We may decline a Book, and we will tell you why if you ask. Approval is for the Books we name; each further Book needs its own Approval.

3.2 What we need from you

You must be at least 18 and able to enter into this agreement, as an individual or for a business. You give us accurate details: your name, country, contact email, payout details and the tax information we are required to collect. You keep them up to date.

3.3 Checks and reporting

Before approving a Book or paying you, we may ask for proof of who you are or of your rights to the Book. We may be required by law to collect information about sellers and report it to tax authorities, for example under EU rules on digital platforms. If we are, we will tell you what we collect and why.

4. Your book and our licence

4.1 You keep the copyright in your Book. Nothing in these terms transfers ownership to us.

4.2 For as long as your Book is listed, you give us a non-exclusive, worldwide licence to host it, display its title, cover, description and any sample you provide, promote it, and deliver it to Readers who buy it. We may make the technical copies and format changes that selling and delivering it needs.

4.3 The licence is non-exclusive. You may sell your Book elsewhere, subject to section 6.

4.4 When a Book is removed, we stop selling it. Readers who already bought it keep the right to read it.

4.5 We never use your Book to train an AI model, ours or anyone else's.

5. How a sale is classified, and your share

5.1 The shares

Type of saleYour share of the Net Amount
Own-audience sale70%
BookPilot-audience sale35%

We keep the rest of the Net Amount as our fee for hosting, checkout, delivery, buyer support and promotion.

5.2 Which sale is which

A sale is an Own-audience sale when the Reader starts the purchase from a link, page or code that identifies you, such as your BookPilot promotion page or a tracked link we give you. Every other sale is a BookPilot-audience sale: for example, a Reader who finds your Book in our catalogue, on our blog, in recommendations or through our own advertising. If it is unclear which a sale is, we decide in good faith and explain our reasons on request. You can ask us to review a classification within 30 days of the statement that shows it.

5.3 An example

This is only an illustration. A Reader pays €12.00; €2.00 of that is VAT and the payment fee is €0.60. The Net Amount is €12.00 − €2.00 − €0.60 = €9.40. If it was an Own-audience sale, your share is 70% = €6.58. If it was a BookPilot-audience sale, your share is 35% = €3.29.

5.4 Prices

You set your Book's price when it is listed, within what we allow. We may refuse or ask you to change a price that is unreasonable or misleading. A price change applies only to sales made after it. Prices shown to Readers are final prices (see section 3.1 of the Terms of service).

5.5 No promise of sales

We do not promise that any Book will sell, or how many copies, or where it will be shown.

You are responsible for checking whether another agreement restricts selling your Book, or a particular edition of it, elsewhere. Amazon KDP Select, for example, requires an enrolled ebook's digital edition to be exclusive to Amazon. Do not list an edition here if that would breach another agreement. We do not check your other agreements and are not responsible if you breach them. If a store or programme requires you to stop selling a Book here, tell us and we will remove it promptly.

8. How Readers buy, and what we do

8.1 We are the seller to the Reader. The contract for each sale is between the Reader and BookPilot, and section 3 of the Terms of service (ordering and payment, delivery, withdrawal and faulty files) applies to it.

8.3 We may promote your Book in our catalogue, on our blog, in recommendations and in advertising, using its title, your name as author, its cover and description. We do not promise any placement or amount of promotion.

9. Refunds and chargebacks

9.1 A Reader can ask for a refund within the Refund Window, as with the direct e-books on bookpilot.org. Readers' legal rights, such as for a faulty file, apply as well.

9.2 When a sale is refunded, your share of it is removed from your Balance. If you have already been paid that share, it is deducted from your next payouts. If no payout is due, for example because your account has ended, you repay that share within 30 days of our request.

9.3 The same applies to a sale that is reversed by a chargeback or lost to payment fraud.

10. Balance and payouts

10.1 Your share of a sale is added to your Balance when the sale completes. It becomes payable once the Refund Window has ended without a refund.

10.2 We pay out once a month everything that is payable, if it comes to at least €20. A smaller amount stays in your Balance and is added to the next month.

10.3 Payouts are made in euros through a payment provider we name when we onboard you, for example Stripe, to the account you give us. You must complete that provider's checks, and its terms apply to your account with it. Fees your own bank or provider charges you are yours.

10.4 With each payout we send you a statement: the sales in it, how each was classified, each Net Amount, any refunds deducted, and your shares.

10.5 We may hold a payout for as long as needed if we reasonably suspect fraud, if there is an unresolved rights complaint or chargeback, if a legal order requires it, or if you have not given us information we are legally required to collect. We tell you why.

10.6 Your Balance is an amount we owe you. It is not a bank deposit and earns no interest.
