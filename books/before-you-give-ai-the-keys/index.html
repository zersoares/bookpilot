Before You Give AI the Keys

A Small Business Guide to AI Agent Permissions, Access Control and Digital Security

AI tools can now read, draft, send, edit and delete on your behalf. That saves time, and it means one wrong instruction or careless permission can reach further than you intended. A practical handbook for getting the benefits without a security department.
