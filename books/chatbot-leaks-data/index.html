When Your AI Chatbot Leaks Data

A Small Business Guide to Chatbot Privacy, Security Testing, and Preventing Customer Data Exposure

Your website chatbot may be answering customers around the clock. But what information can it access? What does it remember? Could it expose private customer information or respond to instructions designed to manipulate it? A practical guide for small businesses that use, or plan to introduce, customer-facing AI chatbots, with a vendor questionnaire, an incident-response plan and a copy-ready toolkit. Test before launch. Protect customer information. Respond with confidence.

What’s inside

  • How website chatbots process and access information
  • Where customer data can be exposed
  • How to assess chatbot providers and their data practices
  • Which GDPR and AI governance questions matter
  • Ten pre-launch security and privacy tests using dummy data
  • How to identify weaknesses before customers are affected
  • What to do if your chatbot exposes sensitive information

Who it’s for. E-commerce owners, SaaS founders, website operators, customer-service managers and small businesses across the UK and Europe. Educational guidance, not legal advice.
