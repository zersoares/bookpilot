The Cyber Insurance Evidence File

A Practical UK and European SME Workbook for Buying Cover, Proving Security Controls and Preparing a Claim

A workbook for small businesses on what cyber insurance really covers, how to answer the application honestly, and how to keep the evidence (MFA, backups, patching, incident contacts) that insurers ask for when a claim lands.
