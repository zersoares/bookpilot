The QR Code Checkpoint

How Businesses Can Stop QR Code Fraud at Their Counters, Menus, Car Parks and Payment Pages

A merchant-side guide to QR code fraud: how sticker replacement and fake payment or donation destinations work, QR phishing by email, static versus dynamic codes, generating codes and redirects securely, signage and placement, verifying payments, talking to customers and handling an incident. Built around five checkpoints: Source, Route, Print, Place and Pay.

What’s inside

  • How QR fraud works against businesses, step by step
  • The five checkpoints: Source, Route, Print, Place, Pay
  • Secure generation, signage and staff briefing templates
  • A 20-point QR audit and incident checklist

Who it’s for. Owners and managers of cafes, shops, venues, car parks, charities and any business that puts QR codes in front of customers. Practical guidance, not legal advice.
