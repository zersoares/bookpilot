The author

Zirlandia Milkovic

Zirlandia Milkovic is a GDPR compliance consultant and the founder of GDPRGard.eu. Zirlandia builds websites, software products and educational material at the intersection of web development and applied artificial intelligence.

The approach across every book is deliberately practical: clear language, honest limits, realistic examples, and exercises, checklists and templates meant to be used rather than admired. The goal is never to turn readers into technologists. It is to make the tools, the rules and the changes around them feel achievable.

Questions

Before you buy

Where can I buy the books?

Paperbacks, hardcovers and Kindle editions are sold on Amazon; each book’s buy button goes straight to its Amazon page. Titles marked “Instant download” are PDFs sold directly here through a secure Stripe checkout, and the free guides download straight away with no sign-up.

Which format should I choose?

Paperback and hardcover suit the longer reference titles you’ll want to annotate and keep at hand. Kindle is the easiest to carry and search. The direct PDFs work on any laptop, tablet or phone, and reach you the moment you pay.

What if a direct PDF isn’t what I expected?

Every direct purchase has a 14-day money-back guarantee. Email hello@gdprgard.eu and you get a full refund, with no forms and no questions.

Are the GDPR and compliance books legal advice?

No. They are educational guides that explain the rules and give you working checklists and templates. For advice on your specific situation, speak to a qualified lawyer or your data-protection officer. The money and mindset books are likewise educational, not financial or psychological advice.

Are any of the books available in other languages?

The five GDPR industry guides (marketing agencies, e-commerce, SaaS, HR, healthcare) are also on Amazon in German, French, Spanish and Italian, and GDPR Made Simple is available in German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese and Croatian through gdprgard.eu. Look for the language links in each book’s details.

Why is a book marked “Coming soon”?

It’s finished and waiting for its store listing. Use the “Email me when it’s out” link on that book’s details and you’ll hear the moment it goes live.
