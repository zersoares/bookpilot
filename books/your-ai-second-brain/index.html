Your AI Second Brain

How to Think, Learn, Create and Organize Your Life with Artificial Intelligence

Most people use AI like a vending machine: ask, receive, move on. This book shows you how to use it as a thinking partner instead, with a practical system for learning, creating, researching, planning and deciding while you keep the judgment.

What’s inside

  • The Context Stack: how to give AI the context that makes answers useful
  • A personal AI operating system across seven lanes: think, learn, create, organize, analyze, decide, execute
  • A 30-day AI learning system and a 30-minute weekly reset
  • The Decision Room, and a research workflow that guards against invented facts

Who it’s for. Entrepreneurs, creators, students and professionals who want AI to help them think, not think for them.
