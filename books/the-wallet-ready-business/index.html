The Wallet-Ready Business

Preparing for the European Digital Identity Wallet: Registration, Verification and Privacy-First Customer Onboarding

A practical guide for businesses that will verify customers or staff in the EU: what the European Digital Identity Wallet is, eIDAS 2.0 in plain words, registering as a relying party, verifying credentials and attributes, selective disclosure and data minimisation, onboarding workflows, GDPR, vendor selection and a readiness checklist.

What’s inside

  • What a business must decide before accepting the wallet
  • Relying-party registration and what to ask customers for
  • Onboarding flows, fallbacks and GDPR points
  • A ten-minute wallet-ready check and readiness checklist

Who it’s for. Financial-services and payments firms, telecoms, professional-service providers and medium-sized businesses that verify identity in the EU. eIDAS rules are amended law; check current texts. Practical guidance, not legal advice.
